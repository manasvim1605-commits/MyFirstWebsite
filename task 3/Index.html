About Cold Coffee

Cold coffee is a smooth and refreshing drink made with coffee, milk, sugar, and ice. Cold coffee isn’t just a drink… it’s a whole personality. It’s for the people who want caffeine and chill, who like their coffee as cool as their attitude and as sweet as their mood on a good day. It’s the magical potion that turns “I can’t do this” into “Okay fine, let’s survive.” Every sip feels like a tiny hug, a mini-vacation, or that one friend who always says, “Don’t worry, you got this.” Cold coffee understands you. It doesn’t judge your deadlines, your laziness, or your habit of checking the fridge for no reason. It simply sits there—creamy, frothy, adorable—and makes you feel like life is 2% easier and 98% tastier. So yes… for coffee lovers, cold coffee is not just a beverage. It’s comfort, comedy, and caffeine—all in one cute, chilly cup.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cold milk
  • 2 tsp instant coffee
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • Vanilla ice cream (optional)

Recipe

1. Add milk, coffee, sugar, and ice cubes to a blender.

2. Blend until smooth.

3. Pour into a glass and add ice cream.
